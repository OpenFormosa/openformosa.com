<div class="post-lang-zh" markdown="1">

開源授權讓每個人都能使用、修改與散布 OpenFormosa 的模型。本政策說明社群期待的使用界線。官方 Demo 與 OpenFormosa 管理的服務必須遵守本政策；自行部署者，也請把本政策視為最低要求，並遵守當地法律。

## 1. 所有模型都不可用於

- 違反法律，包括詐騙、洗錢、侵害著作權或個人資料。
- 騷擾、威脅、仇恨或煽動暴力。
- 製作或散布兒童性剝削內容。
- 冒充真實個人或機構，誤導他人。
- 以假訊息操弄選舉或公共討論。
- 在未告知的情況下，讓人誤以為自己在與真人互動。

## 2. 語音合成（TTS）與聲音複製

- **只能使用你有權使用的聲音。** 使用他人聲音或語者向量前，須取得本人的明確授權。
- **不可冒充。** 不可用合成語音冒充他人進行通話、詐騙、身分驗證或發表言論。
- **標示合成。** 對外發布合成語音時，應標示為 AI 合成。
- BlueMagpie-TTS 內附的預設語者向量已取得本人授權，僅限該模型說明中所列的用途。

## 3. 語音辨識（ASR）

- 不可在未告知或未取得同意的情況下，錄音並轉寫他人的對話。
- 不可用於大規模監控特定個人或群體。

## 4. 文件辨識（OCR）

- 處理含個人資料的文件（發票、表單、證件）時，須有合法依據，並遵守個資法。
- 不可用於蒐集、比對或販售他人的個人資料。

## 5. 回報濫用

發現濫用時，請寄信到 <hello@openformosa.com>。涉及安全漏洞，請依 [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/OpenFormosa/openformosa.com/blob/main/SECURITY.md) 回報。我們可能暫停違反本政策者使用官方 Demo。

<div class="post-lang-en" markdown="1">

Open licenses let anyone use, modify, and redistribute OpenFormosa models. This policy states the boundaries the community expects. It binds official demos and services run by OpenFormosa. If you self-host, treat it as the minimum standard and follow your local laws.

## 1. No model may be used to

- Break the law, including fraud, money laundering, or infringing copyright or personal data rights.
- Harass, threaten, spread hate, or incite violence.
- Create or distribute child sexual abuse material.
- Impersonate real people or organizations to mislead others.
- Manipulate elections or public debate with disinformation.
- Lead people to believe they are talking to a human without disclosure.

## 2. Speech synthesis (TTS) and voice cloning

- **Use only voices you have the right to use.** Get explicit consent before using another person's voice or speaker embedding.
- **Do not impersonate.** Never use synthetic speech to pose as someone in calls, scams, identity checks, or public statements.
- **Label synthetic audio** as AI-generated when you publish it.
- The default speaker embedding shipped with BlueMagpie-TTS is used with the speaker's permission, only for the purposes stated in the model card.

## 3. Speech recognition (ASR)

- Do not record and transcribe people's conversations without notice or consent.
- Do not use it for mass surveillance of individuals or groups.

## 4. Document recognition (OCR)

- Process documents containing personal data (receipts, forms, IDs) only with a lawful basis.
- Do not use it to collect, match, or sell other people's personal data.

## 5. Reporting misuse

Email <hello@openformosa.com>. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/OpenFormosa/openformosa.com/blob/main/SECURITY.md). We may suspend access to official demos for violations.
