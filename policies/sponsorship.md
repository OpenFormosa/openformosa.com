## 1. 原則

支持者讓工作成為可能，但成果屬於所有貢獻者。贊助換到的是感謝與透明，不是方向的控制權。

## 2. 目前接受的支持

| 類型 | 接受方式 |
| --- | --- |
| 運算資源（GPU、雲端額度、Demo 主機） | 以書面協議提供到 OpenFormosa 組織帳號，不撥入個人帳號 |
| 研究支持與討論 | 公開致謝 |
| 開放授權資料，或協助釐清授權 | 須先通過授權與隱私審查 |
| 活動場地與社群資源 | 公開致謝 |

**目前不接受**大眾捐款與現金贊助。原因：《公益勸募條例》第 5 條限定可向大眾募款的主體。之後若接受經費，會先透過立案的非營利代管單位或我們自己登記的法人辦理，並公開收支。

## 3. 支持者會得到

- 在網站[社群頁致謝]({{ '/community/#thanks' | relative_url }})、相關模型卡與技術報告中列名，並說明提供了什麼。
- 使用其名稱與標誌時，須事先取得支持者同意。

## 4. 支持者不會得到

- 決策權、指導小組席次或否決權。
- 成果的獨占授權、搶先取得權或保密權。
- 改變評測方法或結果的權利。評測結果不因任何支持關係而調整或隱藏。
- 以 OpenFormosa 名義背書其產品。

## 5. 揭露與利益衝突

- 每一項支持在開始使用後 30 天內公開列出。
- 支持協議的重點條件（期間、提供內容、使用限制）會公開摘要。
- 若評測對象是支持者的產品或模型，報告中會揭露支持關係。
- 與支持者有利益關係的維護者，應揭露並迴避相關決策。

## 6. 結束支持

任一方可依協議結束支持。已釋出的成果維持開放授權，不因支持結束而撤回。

## 7. 洽談

請寄信到 <sponsors@openformosa.com>。

## 1. Principle

Supporters make the work possible, but results belong to all contributors. Sponsorship earns thanks and transparency, not control.

## 2. Support we accept now

| Type | How |
| --- | --- |
| Compute (GPUs, cloud credits, demo hosting) | Under a written agreement, to OpenFormosa organization accounts, never to personal accounts |
| Research support and discussion | Publicly acknowledged |
| Openly licensed data, or help clarifying licenses | After license and privacy review |
| Venues and community resources | Publicly acknowledged |

We **do not currently accept** public donations or cash sponsorship, because Taiwan's Public Welfare Fundraising Act (Article 5) limits who may raise funds from the public. Before accepting money we will work through a registered nonprofit host or our own registered body, and publish income and spending.

## 3. Supporters receive

- Acknowledgement on the [community page]({{ '/community/#thanks' | relative_url }}), in relevant model cards and technical reports, stating what they provided.
- Use of their name or logo only with their prior consent.

## 4. Supporters never receive

- Decision rights, steering-group seats, or vetoes.
- Exclusive licenses, early access, or confidentiality over results.
- Any say over evaluation methods or results, which are never adjusted or withheld because of support.
- An OpenFormosa endorsement of their products.

## 5. Disclosure and conflicts of interest

- Each form of support is listed publicly within 30 days of first use.
- Key terms (period, what is provided, usage limits) are summarized publicly.
- When we evaluate a supporter's product or model, the report discloses the relationship.
- Maintainers with a stake in a supporter disclose it and recuse themselves from related decisions.

## 6. Ending support

Either side may end support under the agreement. Released work stays under its open license.
