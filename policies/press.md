## 標準簡介

**一句話：** OpenFormosa（開放福爾摩沙）是由社群共同打造的台灣語言開源 AI 模型家族。

**一段話：** OpenFormosa（開放福爾摩沙）是一個開源社群專案，為台灣的語言情境打造小而可部署的 AI 模型，涵蓋斷詞器、1B 基底語言模型、語音合成、語音辨識與文件辨識。所有模型、評測與訓練紀錄都公開，任何人都可以檢查、使用與改進。OpenFormosa 以台灣茄芷袋為象徵，成果屬於所有貢獻者。

## 事實查核

| 成果 | 狀態 | 可引用的事實 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PangolinTokenizer | 已釋出 | 114,688 個 BPE 合併項；PangolinBench 整體 0.485 tokens/字元 |
| Barbet 1B Base | 已釋出 | 1B 參數混合架構；TAIDE-14 上 0.7488 bits/byte，公開 1B 模型中第一 |
| BlueMagpie-TTS | 已釋出 | V1，台灣口音與中英夾雜語音合成；內部測試集 CER 7.44%，相對參考基準 11.45% 降低約 35% |
| BlackBear-ASR | 規劃中 | 尚未釋出權重或 Demo |
| SikaDeer-OCR | 規劃中 | 尚未釋出權重或 Demo |

數字以各模型的技術報告為準。引用時請附上連結。

## 命名與寫法

- 寫作 **OpenFormosa**（一個字，O 與 F 大寫）。中文可寫「開放福爾摩沙」。
- OpenFormosa 是**社群專案**，不是公司，也不屬於任何單一機構。
- 成果的作者是 **OpenFormosa Contributors**。
- 致謝：運算資源來自 Taipei-1；研究支持來自臺大語音實驗室（NTU Speech Lab）與 NTU SPML Lab。支持單位不擁有成果。

## Boilerplate

**One line:** OpenFormosa is a community-built family of open AI models for Taiwan's languages.

**One paragraph:** OpenFormosa is an open-source community project building small, deployable AI models for Taiwan's language contexts, from tokenizers and a 1B base language model to speech synthesis, speech recognition, and document understanding. Models, evaluations, and training notes are public, so anyone can inspect, use, and improve them. Symbolized by the Taiwan Gaji bag, OpenFormosa's results belong to all of its contributors.

## Fact sheet

| Release | Status | Citable facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PangolinTokenizer | Shipped | 114,688 BPE merges; 0.485 tokens/character overall on PangolinBench |
| Barbet 1B Base | Shipped | 1B hybrid architecture; 0.7488 bits/byte on TAIDE-14, first among open 1B models |
| BlueMagpie-TTS | Shipped | V1, Taiwanese-accent, Chinese–English code-switching TTS; CER 7.44% on an internal test set, about 35% lower than the 11.45% reference baseline |
| BlackBear-ASR | Planned | No weights or demo yet |
| SikaDeer-OCR | Planned | No weights or demo yet |

The technical report of each model is the source of truth. Please link to it.
