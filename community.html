Community

Everyone who contributes is OpenFormosa.

OpenFormosa is an open community project. Models, evaluation, documentation, and language review are built by contributors together. We write the rules down so the project does not depend on any single person.

Where we are today

We would rather state the current situation plainly than describe a larger community than we have.

Form

An unincorporated open-source community

OpenFormosa is not yet a registered association or foundation. It runs as an open-source community with a written governance document. We will consider incorporating once enough people take part on a regular basis.

People

A small core, opening up

The number of active maintainers is still small. We are moving account access, release decisions, and review rights to more people, so that no project stops when one person leaves.

Support

Who supports the work

Compute currently comes from Taipei-1, and research support from the NTU Speech Lab and the NTU SPML Lab. We thank them below. Supporters do not own OpenFormosa's results and do not direct community decisions.

Funding

No public fundraising yet

We do not accept public donations at this stage. Under Taiwan's Public Welfare Fundraising Act, only certain registered bodies may raise money from the public. We will publish a transparent arrangement before accepting any funds.

Five working principles

These principles decide the hard cases. When a choice is unclear, we pick the option that keeps the work open and shared.

Open by default

Weights, code, evaluation sets, training notes, and decisions are public unless privacy or licensing prevents it.

The mission outlasts the organization

Released work stays under open licenses that cannot be withdrawn. A future change of legal form cannot close what was already opened.

Credit belongs to contributors

Every release lists the people who made it and what they did. Code is only one kind of contribution.

No one is irreplaceable

Every key account has at least two administrators. Decisions are recorded in public, so anyone can pick up the work.

Supporters do not steer

Sponsors and supporting institutions receive thanks and transparency, not decision rights or exclusive access to results.

How decisions are made

Roles are earned by contribution and can be held by anyone. The full rules live in GOVERNANCE.md.

Contributor

Anyone with an accepted contribution

Code, evaluation, documentation, translation, language review, design, community work, or a well-documented error case. You are listed as a contributor.

Reviewer

Sustained contributors in one project

Nominated by maintainers. Reviewers can review and approve pull requests.

Maintainer

People who keep a project moving

Added by consensus of current maintainers. Maintainers merge changes and publish releases. Inactive maintainers become emeritus and keep their credit.

Steering group

Three to five people, elected yearly

Handles cross-project decisions, shared accounts, and code-of-conduct reports. No more than half may share an employer or laboratory.

Decision rules

  • Everyday changes pass by lazy consensus: no objection within 72 hours.
  • Major changes (licenses, governance, assets, new projects) need a public RFC, at least 7 days of discussion, and two thirds of maintainers.
  • Discussions can happen on Discord. Decisions are recorded on GitHub.

How credit works

  • Contributor lists cover code, docs, evaluation, data review, translation, language review, design, infrastructure, and community work.
  • Technical reports are authored by "OpenFormosa Contributors", with a full list of names and roles.
  • Each release note thanks the people who contributed to that release.
  • You may be listed by a handle instead of your legal name.

Ways to take part

You do not need to train models to help. Pick the path that fits your skills.

Evaluation

Find where models fail

Try the demos and report Taiwan-specific errors in text. Propose benchmark subsets and scoring rules.

Code

Training and tooling

Improve training recipes, tokenizer tools, inference scripts, and reproducibility.

Language

Language review

Review Taigi, Hakka, and Indigenous language output, romanization, and pronunciation. No programming needed.

Docs

Documentation and translation

Clarify model cards, write quickstarts, and keep Chinese and English pages in sync.

Community

Design and community

Welcome newcomers, run meetups, write meeting notes, and improve the website.

Research

Research collaboration

Propose experiments, co-author technical reports, or connect OpenFormosa with your own research.

Your first three steps

  1. Join Discord and say hello in the welcome channel. Pick the topics you care about.
  2. Pick a "good first issue" on GitHub, or report one model error in the error-case channel.
  3. Open a pull request with a Signed-off-by line. A reviewer will help you finish it.

Please do not post other people's voices, documents, identity data, or private records on Discord or GitHub. Describe errors in text instead.

Supporting OpenFormosa

At this stage we accept support that does not involve donations from the public.

What helps now

  • Your time and contributions.
  • Compute or cloud credits, provided to organization accounts under a written agreement, never to a personal account.
  • Openly licensed data, or help clarifying the license of existing data.
  • Research, education, and public-sector collaboration.

What comes later

  • Before accepting money, we will work through a registered nonprofit host or our own registered body.
  • All sponsors and supporters are listed publicly, with what they provided.
  • Sponsorship never buys decision rights, exclusive licenses, or changes to evaluation results.
