它們怎麼接在一起How they fit together

先把文字切好，再讓模型理解，最後變成聲音、文字或答案。評測工具在每一步檢查品質。Split the text first, let the model understand it, then turn it into speech, text, or answers. Benchmarks check quality at every step.

  1. 1 切字Split PangolinTokenizer · ubi_tokenizer 把句子切成模型讀得懂的小片段。PangolinBench 檢查切得好不好。Cut sentences into pieces a model can read. PangolinBench checks the result.
  2. 2 理解Understand Barbet 1B 共同底座。讀懂台灣的文字與長文件。Harness Library 教它照步驟做事。The shared base that reads Taiwan text and long documents. The Harness Library teaches it to follow steps.
  3. 3 說、聽、讀Speak, listen, read BlueMagpie · BlackBear · SikaDeer 接在底座上的任務模型：語音合成、語音辨識、文件辨識。Task models on the base: speech synthesis, speech recognition, and document reading.
